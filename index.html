Segment 1 — Passwords & Multi-Factor Authentication

Mode Read the notes below, then take the quiz.

Key ideas: Use long unique passphrases, never reuse passwords on important accounts, and turn on multi-factor authentication (MFA) wherever offered.

  • Passphrases (several words) are easier to remember yet strong.
  • Use a password manager to generate and store unique passwords.
  • MFA stops most account takeovers even if a password leaks.

Segment 3 — Device Safety & Public Networks

Mode Read the notes below, then take the quiz.

Key ideas: Use updates, lock screens, avoid public Wi-Fi or use VPN/hotspot, never plug in unknown USB devices.

  • Public Wi-Fi exposes traffic; attackers can snoop.
  • Automatic updates patch known vulnerabilities.
  • Lock your screen when you step away.
